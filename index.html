Allergy-aware grocery list

Shop well for everyone at home.

Listwell is a quick shopping list that reads the label for you. Add each person's allergies and diets, scan products in the aisle, and see who a product suits — with an honest “Unknown” when the data isn't there.

  • Type “2 kg tomatoes, milk” — quantities and units are understood (EN, RO, FR)
  • The 14 EU allergens, “may contain” traces, vegan, vegetarian and palm-oil-free
  • Product data from Open Food Facts; everything else stays on your iPhone
  • Several lists, Siri, widgets, receipt scanning and spending overview
