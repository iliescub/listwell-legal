Privacy Policy

Listwell works on your iPhone. There's no account and no Listwell server — your lists, receipts and your household's allergies stay on the device. The only time Listwell goes online is to look up a product barcode in Open Food Facts.

Who we are

Listwell is made by Bogdan Iliescu (“we”, “us”). Under data protection law, including the EU and UK GDPR, we are the controller of the limited personal information described here.

What stays on your iPhone

  • your shopping lists, items, quantities, notes and archived lists
  • household profiles — names you choose, allergies, intolerances and diets. This can be health information, so it never leaves the device
  • products you scanned and the label data Listwell fetched for them
  • receipts you scanned: the photo is read on the device and only the text lines, prices and totals are kept
  • your settings, such as the app language

Reading receipts uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Voice input uses Apple's speech recognition — on the device when your iPhone supports it, otherwise Apple processes the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.

The widget reads a short summary of your current list from a folder only Listwell and its widget can access. Data is included in your iPhone or iCloud backups according to your iOS settings.

Only the barcode is sent — never your profiles, allergies or lists. Like any web request, Open Food Facts sees your IP address and the app name and version.

What Listwell never does

  • Require an account or collect your name, email address or phone number
  • Show ads or include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • Send your household's allergies or diets anywhere
  • Track you across other companies' apps or websites, or sell or share personal information
  • Access your location, contacts or photo library (receipt photos you pick go through Apple's picker)

If you've turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple gives us anonymous crash and performance reports.

Permissions

  • Camera. To scan barcodes and receipts, only while the scanner is open.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition. Only when you tap the microphone to dictate an item.

A note on product data

Listwell is a shopping aid, not medical advice. Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a collaborative database, and can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Always read the label before buying or eating, especially for serious allergies.

How long information is kept

On your iPhone, until you delete it or delete the app. You can export everything as a file under Settings → Export Data. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 12 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. Listwell doesn't send us personal information, so the only processing we do is answering your messages, based on our legitimate interest in helping you. To make a request, email support@getwellfit.app; we reply within one month.

Children

Listwell isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

This website

No cookies, analytics or third-party content. The host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs for security.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page and its date if anything changes. If a change would ever send anything from your iPhone to us, we'll ask you in the app first.
