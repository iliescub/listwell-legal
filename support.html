Lists
How do I add items quickly?

Type in the box at the bottom of the list, for example “2 kg tomatoes, milk, 6 eggs”. Quantities and units (kg, g, l, ml, pcs, packs — also in Romanian and French) are understood. You can also say “Add to my Listwell list” to Siri.

How do I use more than one list?

Tap the list name at the top to switch lists or create a new one. Each list can be checked for everyone in the household or only some people.

I deleted an item by mistake.

Tap Undo on the banner that appears right after deleting.

Allergies and diets
How does the allergy check work?

Add each person in Settings → Household Profiles with their allergies (the 14 EU allergens), whether to avoid “may contain” traces, and diets. When you scan a product, Listwell compares its label data from Open Food Facts with each profile.

What do OK, Caution, Not suitable and Unknown mean?

Not suitable: the data lists something to avoid. Caution: it may contain traces. OK: nothing to avoid was found in the data. Unknown: the data is missing or incomplete — read the label yourself. Listwell never says a product is safe; always read the label.

A product's information is wrong or missing.

Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a free collaborative database. Tap “View or correct on Open Food Facts” on the product to fix it for everyone.

Your data
Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no Listwell server. Export a copy any time in Settings → Export Data.

Does Listwell work offline?

Yes. Lists, profiles and products you scanned before all work offline. Looking up a new barcode needs a connection.
